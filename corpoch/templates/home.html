Corpo

Corpo is a Clone Hero tournament organizer and stats collector. For tournaments it's used in, it pulls all play stats from screenshots.

All of the data collected is then aggregated and made easily findable through this site or the API.

About

Corpo is a passion project made by competitive Clone Hero players. Its primary aim is to make Clone Hero tournaments easier to run, and make their data easily available.

It will always remain open-source, free to use, and collect as little information as possible.
