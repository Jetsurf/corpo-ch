Corpo FAQ

There are many details with Corpo alone to cover. Clone Hero tournaments in-particular also have mutliple aspects that can cause confusion.

This page aims to answer frequently seen questions for both Corpo, and Clone Hero tournaments.

General
New player? Best things to do.

- Ensure auto-screenshots are turned on. Only in-game screenshots will provide full stats. Keeping this on will ensure all your matches are counted properly.

- Don't change your in-game name for matches played for a tournament. This ensures the bot will properly accept your screenshots.

- Add the dedicated servers (below) to your game. In the future, playing matches on Corpo's CH servers may make more features possible.

How do I get a tournament tracked?

At this time, it's not trivial to setup Corpo for tournament use without site administrator help. This may change in the future.

DM/Contact Jetsurf or join the CORPo Offices discord.

What is Live-Matches for?

Live-Matches shows any matches that are actively being played and updates as the match progresses. The primary use-case, however, is for streamers.

The color options are intended to be used as chroma-keying to be live-overlayed streaming tools to show live-match stats on-stream.

Stats Tracking/Details
Excess-Hits/Overstrums are Wrong? (CH 1.0 Only)

Excess-Hits in Clone Hero 1.0 aren't actually provided cleanly in the data Corpo gets.

The stats on excess-hits are provided as a best-effort. Corpo does attempt to use OCR to extract them on screenshots where it's visible. However, not all screenshots actually show this stat.

How is the info from a screenshot pulled out?

In-game taken screenshots embed most of your play-stats into the screenshot itself. The data isn't visible, but can be extracted.

CH Steg Reader by GMS is able to read this data, and show the stats in-more detail than what's visually shown.

This is why in-game screenshots are important. Out-of-game taken screenshots do not have this data.
